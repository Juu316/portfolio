Setting `draft: true` in the frontmatter keeps a post out of the build
entirely — no page, no sitemap entry, no RSS item, no link in the writing
list. Remove that line (or set it to `false`) when the post is ready.

Use this to keep work-in-progress in the repo without publishing it.
